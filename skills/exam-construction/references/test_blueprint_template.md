## Recommended cognitive levels

- Recall: terminology, names, stages, definitions.
- Understanding: explain meaning, distinguish concepts, identify examples.
- Application: apply a principle or theory to a familiar case.
- Analysis: diagnose mechanisms, compare competing explanations, identify evidence.
- Evaluation: judge intervention, critique method, defend a decision.
- Creation: design an activity, plan an assessment, generate an intervention proposal.

## Weighting rules

- Instructional time and course objectives should determine weights.
- Applied objectives require applied items.
- Avoid assigning high score weight to trivia or single textbook sentences.
- Include a small number of integrative items if the course emphasizes conceptual synthesis.
